Story

A prestige psychological thriller about visibility, confession, and the cost of performance.

Controlling Idea

When the desire to be seen replaces the desire to be loved, truth becomes performance — and performance becomes survival.

World & Tone

Chicago as character. Mixed-media language: vlogs, bodycam, archive. Beauty as discomfort; camera as confession.
